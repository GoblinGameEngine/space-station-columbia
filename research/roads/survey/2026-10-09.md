# Road survey, 2026-10-09

The user: "send npcs in cars to drive all of the major roads and bridges at once. Have them report any misalignments
and anomalies ... Collect a comprehensive list of problems/anomalies, tell me what you learned and then without
permission, begin corrections."

## How it was run
`RoadSurvey` (`remake/tools/road_survey.gd`; `godot4 --path . -- --roadsurvey [--survey-all] [--quit-after-survey]`)
stands down every other road user (the traffic, the people, the trams, the parked fleet). It then lays out
**1,692 surveyor cars**:
- **886 on the major roads** (highway, county, main). Each road's pieces are chained by name; the cars are spaced every
  6 km, each driving its 6 km stretch, one set each way.
- **806 on the bridges**: all 11 great bridges and 392 crossings, one car each way, from 0.5 km before the deck to
  0.5 km past it.

They drove **1,227 km in 226 s**, all at once, a metre at a time in the right-hand lane. They measured the surface the
game draws: the graded ground (`MapTerrain.elevation`), a great bridge's deck (`GreatBridges.deck_h`) or a crossing's
deck. These are the game's own data, so a surveyor 20 km from the player measures exactly what would be there.

Each anomaly runs the evaluation (`RoadSurvey._evaluate`). It looks at what surrounds the spot: a bridge end, a levelled
pad, a junction, the natural slope, water, a great bridge's line. From that it records the cause, the fix and the
severity, so no one has to look at the spot to know what it is. Results go to `user://road_survey.json`; read them with
`python3 tools/traffic_reports.py --survey`.

- 16,100 sign pairs stand within 1.2 m of each other, facing the same way at the same height; 935 stand on the same

## What we learned
1. **The road lines themselves are the biggest fault.** Lines traced off a raster carry its staircase, and corners were
   never rounded to a radius a car turns. Every downstream system inherits that: the ribbons, the traffic's routes, the
   signs placed by heading.
2. **Great bridges and their roads disagreed.** The span is built straight between its ends; the road went on curving
   under it. Small crossings already had the road eased straight across (`placement.snap_crossings`); the great bridges
   never did.
3. **Signs were placed per road, never against each other or against the other roads.** At a junction, a sign set
   behind one road's kerb landed in the cross street, or on top of the next sign.
4. **The grading is mostly sound.** `road_profile` is grade-limited across the network. The steps and humps sit where
   other layers meet it: bridge ends, levelled pads, junction levelling.
5. **The checks need checking too.** The crossing frame and hidden signs at different heights were false alarms; the
   flooding was real, but its first cause was wrong.

## Corrections begun
- `tools/road_fix.py`, the general fixer, now in `tools/regen_world.sh` before placement. For every road of every
  class it de-stairs (Douglas–Peucker, 0.7 m), rounds corners to the class's least radius, joins near-miss ends, and
  lays great bridges' roads straight across their spans. Junction points are pinned. First run: 1.52 M points become
  0.86 M, 6,498 corners rounded, 533 ends joined, 8 bridge roads straightened.
- `tools/road_furniture.py`:
  - a carriageway pass (no sign within 0.4 m of any road's kerb line);
  - a spacing pass (duplicates dropped, blades onto their stop sign's post, crowding signs moved along the kerb or
    dropped);
  - stop signs without a junction dropped;
  - hemmed signs slid along the kerb.
- `NpcPlaces.side_offset`: a driver's lane never puts the car over the kerb (a 3 m alley is driven down its middle).
- The whole chain from the road lines on is regenerating; the survey runs again on every road (`--survey-all`).

### What the rounds taught

- **Some anomalies were the survey's own.**
  - *Dangling ends:* the check measured from probes 4 m around a road's tip. At a joined T-junction the tip's own
    road hides the true 0 m, so about 2,800 joined ends read as "4 m short". The check now measures the true
    distance to the other road's centreline.
  - *Bridge offsets:* a bridge's surveyor measured every crossing it met against its own bridge, giving offsets of
    9–180 m. The check now measures against the deck underfoot.
  - *Signs in the road:* a sign just past a road's end counted as inside it.
  - *Under-passes:* streets passing under the Miami Gateway Bridge's approach "fell" 14 m off it. A deck that runs
    across the road's way, far above it, is now one the road passes under. Judged by height alone, College Rd's own
    lane counted as "under" its bridge in round 3: round 3's 67 floods, a false alarm.
- **Hidden signs were pay stations.** 85% were 1.3 m pay stations against 2.1 m stop posts. The spacing pass skipped
  any two signs more than 0.6 m apart in height. Two posts this close crowd each other at any height.
- **Neighbouring roads leaked onto each other.** On a hillside, a road's grading (cut and fill reaching up to 14 m)
  blended into the next road's lane: 1,300 single-road steps and cambers. A road's own carriageway now takes its own
  height, and other roads blend in only where carriageways overlap or off them. The fade runs over 4.5 m: over 1 m,
  side roads stepped 0.12–0.16 m where they met a main road (round 3's 628 junction steps).
- **Corner rounding alone doesn't make a road drivable.** A fillet shrinks to fit short legs, and a run of short legs
  turns tighter than any one corner shows. `road_fix.ease_bends` holds a whole road to its class's least radius:
  - it densifies only the stretches that turn too fast;
  - it relaxes their points toward their neighbours' middle;
  - junction and deck points stay pinned;
  - no point moves more than 6–8 m;
  - after placement, no point moves into a building's footprint.
- **A clean-up can cut the network.** Some map roads meet the next one by a spur out to the junction and back.
  Deleting spikes took those spurs, and the walking and driving network went from 23 pieces to 34 (Marlowe Rd's 127
  junctions cut off). Junction points are now pinned before any clean-up. Road ends within 8 m of another road are
  joined however they point; Lakeshore Rd was 6.4 m short of S 3rd St, which cut its bridge off from the town.
- **The great bridges' deck line was smoothed twice.** `GreatBridges` re-rounded the road's line over 20 m and drifted
  up to 3 m off it, so the lane ran on and off the deck. `road_fix` now owns the line's shape; the deck rounds over
  4 m.
