# Road signs

## United States (MUTCD)
- **Regulatory:** stop (red octagon), yield (red and white inverted triangle), speed limit (white rectangle, black legend).
- **Warning:** yellow diamond with black symbols. Guide signs are green, services blue, recreation brown, work zones orange, and school zones fluorescent yellow-green.
- **Statutory limits:** 25 mph in residential and school districts, 55 mph on rural highways, 70 mph on rural interstates. The 24 x 30 in speed sign is standard at 25–35 mph.
- Signs stand on the right-hand side of the road.

## Canada (TAC MUTCDC; Québec has its own)
- Signs are metric (km/h) and rely more on symbols than words.
- Signs are bilingual where required (STOP / ARRÊT).
- Overhead pedestrian crossing signs with a flashing beacon are common.
- Shapes and colours are otherwise close to the US.

## United Kingdom (TSRGD, Worboys 1965)
- **Circles give orders** (red ring = prohibition, blue disc = mandatory). **Triangles warn** (red border). **Rectangles inform.**
- Direction signs:

  | Road | Sign colours |
  |---|---|
  | Motorway | white on blue |
  | Primary route | white on green, yellow route numbers |
  | Local | black on white |

- The Transport typeface, set in mixed case.
- Speed limits:
  - 30 mph in built-up areas with street lighting (a red ring on a white disc);
  - the national limit (a white disc with a black diagonal) out of town.
- Signs are mounted at least 2.4 m clear over footways.

## Continental Europe (Vienna Convention 1968)
- Danger signs are red-bordered triangles (white or yellow field). Prohibitions are red circles, obligations blue circles.
- **Priority road** is a yellow diamond. Direction signs are blue, green or yellow by road class.
- **Town entry and exit signs** set the urban default of 50 km/h. The rural default is 80–100 km/h.
- Typefaces vary: DIN 1451 in Germany, Caractères in France.
