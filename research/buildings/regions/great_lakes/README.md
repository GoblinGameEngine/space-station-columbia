# Region: Great Lakes

- **Who**: the founders' source area (research/bible/01_world.md: the NAIC's Great Lakes states and provinces).
- **Where**: Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, Minnesota, Lake Erie Pennsylvania, western New York,
  Ontario.
- **Climate**: IECC zones 5A (most), 6A (north Michigan, Wisconsin, south Minnesota), 7 (far north). Frost line
  32-80 in; ground snow 20-60+ psf with lake-effect belts.
- **Code family**: BOCA until 2000, then the I-codes; Wisconsin's own dwelling code; Ontario's NBC-based OBC.

Status 2026-10-05:
- **Done**: codes, styles, compatibility, futures, bibliography.
- **Corpus**: 13 Commons buckets plus 24 Lucas County assessor cards.
- **Tokens**: 26 buildings annotated so far (the pilot). The method continues through the corpus and the existing
  remake/reference sets.
- **Lots**: 21 Wisconsin areas measured, ~2,900 lots with tokens.
- **Centres and industry**: 10 Wisconsin examples measured.
