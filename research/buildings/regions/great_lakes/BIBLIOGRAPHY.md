# Great Lakes: what we referenced and why (2026-10-05)

Each source: **what it is**, **what we took**, **why this one**. Links are in the topic files. "Primary" means the
authority itself (a code, a government record, the original paper); "secondary" means a summary we relied on where
the primary is paywalled or unreadable.

## Style, compatibility, futures (02-04)

| Source | What we took | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **McAlester, *A Field Guide to American Houses*** | Form before style | The standard identification reference used by preservation offices |
| **Kniffen, "Folk Housing: Key to Diffusion" (1965)**; the I-house literature (McLean County History, Annals of Iowa) | Folk types carried by migration | The founding paper of US folk-housing geography; the I-house is the Corn Belt's farm house |
| Ontario Heritage Trust; Niagara Now; Brampton style guide | The Ontario Gothic cottage | Canadian heritage bodies, for the Ontario shore |
| **Gowans, *The Comfortable House*** | 1890-1930 types and catalogue sources | The scholarly treatment of the foursquare and bungalow era |
| Buffalo Architecture and History; HUD "Kit Homes"; Roanoke pattern-book history | Catalogue and kit houses, Sears numbers | Regional (Buffalo) and government summaries |
| **Longstreth, *The Buildings of Main Street*** (via THC, Naperville survey) | Commercial composition types | The standard typology for Main Street surveys |
| **Venturi, Scott Brown, Izenour, *Learning from Las Vegas*** | Duck and decorated shed; signs on the strip | The foundational study of car-oriented commercial form |
| **Stiny and Mitchell (1978); Koning and Eizenberg (1981)** | Styles as generative grammars; the Prairie house grammar | Primary papers; Koning and Eizenberg is set in our region |
| **Müller, Wonka et al., "Procedural Modeling of Buildings" (2006)**; Schwarz and Müller | Mass, facade split, detail; the CGA method | Primary; the method behind CityEngine |
| Appleton; **Hildebrand, *Origins of Architectural Pleasure*** | Prospect-refuge, enticement, order and complexity | The evolutionary-preference literature applied to buildings |
| **Nasar, *The Evaluative Image of the City***; Stamps facade complexity; U. Sydney facade-preference model | What people like: upkeep, order, organised complexity | Empirical preference studies |
| **Groat, "Contextual Compatibility in Architecture"** (U. Michigan) | Infill that repeats massing and composition is preferred | The empirical test of compatibility |
| Sioux Falls, South Dakota, Ridgewood NJ, Chattanooga, St. Paul design-review documents | The visual compatibility factors | Primary municipal rules; St. Paul is in the region |
| BSC, GBA, ASES, DOE (Passive House in cold climates) | Energy-driven form in zones 5-7 | Building-science authorities |
| Dezeen, ProBuilder, Harvard GSD (The Plan) | Mass timber, prefab, 3D printing | Trade and academic press on current trends |
| Raconteur, Axios, CB Insights, ICSC, STO, Untapped, Planetizen | Dark stores, mall reuse, office conversion | Industry and planning press: the trends are too new for scholarship |
