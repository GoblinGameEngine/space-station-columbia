# 2. The six large cities

Each city's rules are the station's own, modelled on its basis city (see README).

## Port Carrow (law modelled on Cleveland, Ohio; Port Carrow Codified Ordinances (PCCO))

the harbour capital of the North Shore: dense grid, lake winds, a downtown of brick blocks.

**Transit:** Port Carrow Lakeshore Transit (PCLT). Stop flag: navy rectangle, white tram symbol in a white ring, a bronze stripe at the foot; route numbers in white boxes (rectangle 450 x 600 mm on its own post).

**Downtown parking (PCCO 451):** meters yes, 8 AM - 6 PM Mon-Sat, 2-hour limit, pay stations by the block.
**Residential parking:** both sides where the street is 9.1 m or wider, one side from 7.3 m, none narrower; overnight allowed; at most 48 hours; sweeping: one weekday morning a month, R7-2 'NO PARKING 8 AM - 11 AM 2ND TUE'.
**Special:** Emergency Routes (R7-203): no parking when the Marshals declare an emergency; tow-away; Harbour loading zones 6 AM - 10 AM on the dock streets; Regatta Week: the Lakeshore Road kerbs closed (temporary R7-1 hoods).
**Angle parking:** no. **Permit zones:** Côte quarter residential permit (R7-108 'EXCEPT BY PERMIT').

**Sidewalks (PCCO 511):** downtown at least 3.7 m from kerb to building; residential 1.5 m walks behind a 1.8 m tree lawn; both sides of every street in the city; on one side at least of county roads within the limits.

**Speeds (km/h):** residential_kmh 40, business_kmh 40, school_kmh 30, alley_kmh 25

## Kessler (law modelled on Columbus, Ohio; Kessler City Code (KCC))

the capital: the Assembly Hall district, government offices, a planned grid.

**Transit:** Capital Area Tramways (CAT). Stop flag: red roundel on a white disc, a white tram symbol on the bar, the stop name below in a white panel (round 600 mm disc over a 300 x 450 mm name panel).

**Downtown parking (KCC 2105):** meters yes, 8 AM - 10 PM Mon-Sat, 3-hour limit, pay stations; the Assembly District 1-hour on session days.
**Residential parking:** both sides where the street is 8.5 m or wider, one side from 6.7 m, none narrower; overnight allowed; at most 72 hours; sweeping: none (the city sweeps by schedule, signed street by street).
**Special:** the 72-hour rule: no vehicle may stay on a public street longer than 72 hours (the station's rule, after Columbus's practice); Assembly District permit parking for residents (R7-108 'EXCEPT BY PERMIT'); Charter Day: the parade route closed from 6 AM (temporary R7-1).
**Angle parking:** no. **Permit zones:** Assembly District, Old Kessler.

**Sidewalks (KCC 4123):** downtown at least 4.3 m from kerb to building; residential 1.5 m walks behind a 1.5 m tree lawn; both sides of every street; new subdivisions build them with the street (Complete Streets policy).

**Speeds (km/h):** residential_kmh 40, business_kmh 40, school_kmh 30, alley_kmh 25

## Solana Point (law modelled on Cincinnati, Ohio; Solana Point Municipal Code (SPMC))

the South Shore's boom city: hills down to the sea, stairs between streets, crowded kerbs.

**Transit:** Solana Metro Tranvía (SMT). Stop flag: sun-yellow pennant shape, black tram symbol, the stop name in Spanish and English (pennant 450 x 650 mm).

**Downtown parking (SPMC 507):** meters yes, 9 AM - 9 PM Mon-Sat, 1 PM - 9 PM Sun on Pier Plaza, 2-hour limit, pay stations.
**Residential parking:** both sides where the street is 9.8 m or wider, one side from 7.9 m, none narrower; overnight allowed; at most 48 hours; sweeping: none; on hill streets narrower than 26 ft, parking on the downhill side only.
**Special:** Rush-hour arterials: NO PARKING 7 - 9 AM / 4 - 6 PM toward downtown (R7-2 + R7-201P TOW-AWAY ZONE); Hill streets: wheels turned to the kerb on grades over 5 %; Suns game days: the Sun Bowl streets by permit.
**Angle parking:** no. **Permit zones:** Pier Plaza residents, the Sun Bowl blocks on game days.

**Sidewalks (SPMC 721):** downtown at least 3.7 m from kerb to building; residential 1.5 m walks behind a 1.2 m tree lawn; both sides; public stairs where streets are too steep to join (steps 7 in max, handrails both sides).

**Speeds (km/h):** residential_kmh 40, business_kmh 40, school_kmh 30, alley_kmh 25

## Harrow Falls (law modelled on Dayton, Ohio; Harrow Falls Revised Code of General Ordinances (HFRCGO))

the mill town at the Falls: the Works, the river, workers' terraces, the bluff.

**Transit:** Kettle Valley Transit (KVT). Stop flag: green shield (the old mill-town crest), white tram symbol, a white band with the stop name (shield 500 x 650 mm).

**Downtown parking (HFRCGO 76):** meters yes, 8 AM - 6 PM Mon-Fri, 2-hour limit, coin and card meters on posts.
**Residential parking:** both sides where the street is 11.0 m or wider, one side from 8.5 m, none narrower; overnight allowed; at most 72 hours; sweeping: none.
**Special:** Flood Routes (R7-203, aboard: FLOOD ROUTE): no parking when the Kettle runs high; tow-away (after the Kettle Flood, VY 447); Works shift change: the Falls Yard streets NO PARKING 5:30 - 7 AM and 2:30 - 4 PM; Trucks over 4.5 t: not on residential streets overnight.
**Angle parking:** yes. **Permit zones:** none.

**Sidewalks (HFRCGO 92):** downtown at least 3.0 m from kerb to building; residential 1.2 m walks behind a 1.2 m tree lawn; both sides in the city; one side on the bluff roads.

**Speeds (km/h):** residential_kmh 40, business_kmh 40, school_kmh 30, alley_kmh 25

## Brightwater (law modelled on Toledo, Ohio; Brightwater Municipal Code (BMC))

the pleasure town of the North Shore: the boardwalk, the arcades, summer crowds.

**Transit:** Brightwater Boardwalk Trams (BBT). Stop flag: teal lollipop disc with a cream rim, white tram symbol, the stop name on a cream bar (disc 550 mm over a bar 600 x 150 mm).

**Downtown parking (BMC 335):** meters yes, 10 AM - 10 PM, Memorial Day to Labor Day (the summer season), 3-hour limit, seasonal pay stations.
**Residential parking:** both sides where the street is 9.1 m or wider, one side from 7.3 m, none narrower; overnight allowed; at most 48 hours; sweeping: none.
**Special:** Angle parking on the Boardwalk approaches (back-in angle, 45 degrees); Summer-season beach permit lots; Emergency Routes (R7-203) on the Coast Highway.
**Angle parking:** yes. **Permit zones:** Beach blocks (summer).

**Sidewalks (BMC 521):** downtown at least 3.7 m from kerb to building; residential 1.5 m walks behind a 1.2 m tree lawn; both sides; the Boardwalk counts as the sidewalk of the streets that end at it.

## Every city: tram stop zones

No stopping or parking within 45.7 m either side of a tram stop, on both kerbs; R7-107 NO PARKING (tram symbol) at both ends of the zone, arrows pointing into it; no parking lane line through the zone. Basis: the station's own rule (TCRP Report 19 bus zones, knowledge).
