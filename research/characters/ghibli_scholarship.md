# Bibliography: Ghibli style, character animation, and NPC generation

Status key:
- **[local]** = a PDF in `reference/papers/` (gitignored);
- **[read]** = read or summarised for this research;
- **[listed]** = found in a bibliography and not yet read, recorded for the literary phase and
  later.

---

## A. Ghibli: art style, colour, animation (core)

- **Animation Obsessive, "The Artist Who Colored Ghibli"** (on Michiyo Yasuda). [read]
  https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/the-artist-who-colored-ghibli
  Yasuda's method: several hundred colours per film, semi-natural and faded colours, a palette
  per time of day, and shadow as a chosen colour. → `ghibli_style.md` §2.
- **Animation Obsessive, "Hayao Miyazaki on Running"**. [read]
  https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/hayao-miyazaki-on-running
  Miyazaki's running cycles (4 frames on ones, 6 on twos, 8) and his insistence that each
  character runs as themselves. → `ghibli_style.md` §4.
- **Animation Obsessive, "Time and Space the Miyazaki Way"**. [read]
  https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/time-and-space-the-miyazaki-way
  Timing, pauses and spatial staging.
- **Sakuga Blog, "Isao Takahata: Complete Retrospective"** (2018). [read]
  https://blog.sakugabooru.com/2018/04/16/isao-takahata-complete-retrospective/
  Takahata's realism: prescored dialogue and facial musculature in *Only Yesterday*, and the
  layout system. Useful on how restrained faces still act.
- **Brooks, M. R. (2022). "Pause and presence: Ma in Miyazaki's animation."** *Animation
  Studies* 17(1), 44–59. [read: summary]. Ma (間) as held stillness. → idle design.
- **Peres, Cátia (2017). "Out of gravity: Physics in animation and in the films of Hayao
  Miyazaki."** *Animation Studies* 12. [read]
  https://journal.animationstudies.org/article/id/91/
  Weight, gravity and flight: why Ghibli characters feel solid.
- **Law, Jo (2022). "The kraft of labour, labour as craft: Hayao Miyazaki's images of work."**
  *Animation: An Interdisciplinary Journal* 17(2), 195–208. [read: abstract]
  https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/17468477221092357
  Work as a central image, and the argument for occupation-first NPCs.
- **Crombie, Zoe (2021). "The spectacular mundane in the films of Studio Ghibli."** *Journal of
  Anime and Manga Studies* 2, 1–26. [local] [read]
  https://iopn.library.illinois.edu/journals/jams/article/view/507
  Everyday acts (cooking, cleaning, eating) staged as spectacle. → NPC work and idle libraries.
- **Napier, Susan (2018). *Miyazakiworld: A Life in Art*.** Yale University Press. [read: reviews]
  The standard critical biography.
- **Lamarre, Thomas (2009). *The Anime Machine: A Media Theory of Animation*.** University of
  Minnesota Press. [read: reviews] "Animetism" and layered compositing; the theoretical basis for
  flat layers and sliding planes in anime.
  https://www.upress.umn.edu/9780816651559/the-anime-machine/
- **Denison, Rayna (2023). *Studio Ghibli: An Industrial History*.** Palgrave. [listed]
  Production structure and division of labour (colour design, key animation, in-betweening).
- **Miyazaki, Hayao. *Starting Point 1979–1996*** (Viz, 2009). [listed] Miyazaki's essays and
  notes on animation, character and observation.
- ***Studio Ghibli Layout Designs*** (exhibition catalogue, 2008). [listed] The layout
  drawings, with the staging and the character-to-background relationship.
- **Kondō Yoshifumi**: interviews on *Whisper of the Heart* ("understated solidity"). [read]
  https://discoverghibli.com/what-we-wanted-to-convey-in-this-film-yoshifumi-kondo/
- **"Toonz, Toon Shaders and Studio Ghibli"**. [read]
  https://blog.alltheanime.com/toonz-toon-shaders-and-studio-ghibli/
  Ghibli's CG: the Toonz digital ink-and-paint pipeline, and the toon shaders (Softimage,
  Michael Arias) used from *Princess Mononoke*.
- **Michiyo Yasuda** (Wikipedia, and the obituary in Vice). [read]
- **Mechademia: Second Arc 18(2)**, a Studio Ghibli issue (2026). [listed]
  https://www.animemangastudies.com/2024/04/24/call-for-papers-mechademia-second-arc-18-2-studio-ghibli/

## B. 3D anime rendering

- **Motomura, Junya (2015). "GuiltyGearXrd's Art Style: The X Factor Between 2D and 3D."** GDC
  2015. [local] [read]
  https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1022031/GuiltyGearXrd-s-Art-Style-The ; slides:
  https://www.ggxrd.com/Motomura_Junya_GuiltyGearXrd.pdf
  Edited normals, vertex-colour shading thresholds, tint textures, inverted-hull and UV
  lines, limited animation. → `ghibli_style.md` §3.2.
- **Petrovic, Fujito, Williams, Finkelstein (2000). "Shadows for Cel Animation."** SIGGRAPH
  2000. [local] [read]
  https://gfx.cs.princeton.edu/proj/cel_shadows/petrovic2000.pdf
  Designed shadow shapes, and computing them from a rough 3D proxy.
- **VRM / MToon** toon material parameters (shade colour, shade shift, outline width). [read]
