# Gait reference numbers

These are normative adult walking kinematics from clinical gait analysis (Perry, *Gait
Analysis*; Winter, *Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement*; the University of
Missouri PM&R "Normal Gait" teaching notes; OUHSC gait kinematics pages). They are
typical values: the generator treats them as the neutral centre and personality, emotion, age and
body move away from them.

## The cycle
A stride is two steps. Stance is ~60% of the cycle for each leg and swing ~40%, with two periods of
double support (~10% each). The key events per leg are: initial contact (heel strike) →
loading response → mid-stance → terminal stance (heel off) → pre-swing (toe off) → initial
swing → mid-swing → terminal swing.

## Sagittal joint angles (approximate)
| | initial contact | loading | mid-stance | terminal stance | pre-swing | initial swing | mid-swing | terminal swing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hip (flexion +) | +25–30° | +25° | 0° | −10° (extension) | 0° | +15° | +25° | +25–30° |
| knee (flexion +) | 0–5° | **+15–20°** (shock absorption) | ~5° | ~5° | +40° | **+60°** (peak) | +25° | 0° |
| ankle (dorsiflexion +) | 0° (heel strike, toe up) | −10° (foot lowers) | +10° | +10° → heel off | **−20°** (push-off) | −10° | 0° | 0° |

## Pelvis and trunk
- **Pelvic rotation** (transverse): about ±4–5° (the swing side advances with the leg).
- **Pelvic obliquity / list** (frontal): ±4–5°. The pelvis drops on the swing side during
  single support; this is the "hip sway" that is larger in women's walks.
- **Pelvic tilt** (sagittal): ~2–4° each way.
- **Lateral shift:** the body moves ~2–5 cm toward the stance leg.
- **Vertical bob:** ~4–5 cm total in adults, lowest at double support and highest at mid-stance.
- **Thorax:** rotates opposite the pelvis (counter-rotation), about half the amplitude.
- **Arms:** swing opposite the legs, ~±15–20° at the shoulder in a normal walk, with the elbow at
  ~10–20° of flexion and more on the forward swing. Arm swing grows with speed.

## Step geometry and tempo
- **Step width:** ~5–13 cm between the feet' centres; the feet land near a line under the body
  (Muybridge).
- **Step length:** ~0.4 × height at normal speed; stride = two steps.
- **Cadence:** ~100–120 steps per minute; comfortable speed ~1.2–1.4 m/s for adults, slower
  with age (~1.0 m/s at 80), faster and shorter-stepped for children.

## Age and body
- **Elders:** shorter steps, wider base, less knee flexion in swing, forward trunk lean,
  reduced arm swing, longer double support, flatter foot contact (less heel strike).
- **Children:** higher cadence, relatively longer steps for their size, more vertical bounce,
  and a wider base under ~4 years.
- **Heavier bodies:** a wider base, more lateral sway, a slower cadence.

## Muybridge observations
(`principles.md` §1): feet near one line; pelvis dropping on the swing side; a straight front
knee at contact with the toe up; the back foot up on its toes; a low foot pass; modest arm swing
across the body; a level head.
