# Traffic: rules of the road, rule-breaking, and the people behind it

1. `01_ohio_rules.md`: Ohio Revised Code Chapter 4511, as drivers apply it to what they perceive.
2. `02_violation_rates.md`: national and Ohio rates of speeding, rolling stops, red lights, passing on the right, tailgating and failing to yield, plus the felony base rate.
3. `03_psychology.md`: self-control, sensation seeking, anger and the Big Five, and the link between risky driving and wider offending.
4. `04_model.md`: the method (TrafficSigns, RoadDriver, NpcTraffic, the trams) and the calibrated generator weights.
5. `05_physical_driving.md`: near the player, NPC cars drive on their wheels (VehicleBody3D), steered and braked by their drivers' decisions; drivable routes; parked cars as obstacles; getting into parked cars.

The rule throughout: drivers react to signs, markings and other road users, never to per-road rules.
