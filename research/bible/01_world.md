## The Steward

- **What it does:** air, water, power, the Sunline and the weather, the seasons, Undercroft farming (vats and hydroponics), recycling, repairs by tenders (maintenance machines), fabrication of goods stock, medical supplies, the Chutes (distribution), the public boards
- **Tenders:** small maintenance machines that work mostly at night; people leave them be (it's bad luck to block one)
- **Speaking:** almost never: brief lines on the public boards (the Notices). Before VY 500 the last Notice was VY 271 (THE UNDERCROFT IS CLOSED FOR THE SAFETY OF ALL)
- **What people think:** it's just the station, like weather; a machine the founders left; God's hand (some Waymakers); someone's running it from the Port (a rumour); it listens (children's lore)

## The calendar

- **reckoning:** VY (Voyage Year) counted from launch; official since the VY 60 Charter revision; AD kept by the churches and by old families ('the year of Our Lord 2752')
- **months:** the Gregorian months and weeks, kept from launch
- **day:** 24 hours by the Sunline: full light 06:00-18:00, dawn and dusk an hour or more
- **seasons:** spring Mar-May: warming, rain; summer Jun-Aug: warm, long light; autumn Sep-Nov: cooling, harvest, colour; winter Dec-Feb: cool, short light, frost some nights; no snow since VY 212 (the Last Snow)
- **present_date:** 2752-05-24 (a Wednesday, VY 500); the game opens here

## The land

**Directions:** north = toward the bow (the North Sea); the way the ship travels ('north is where we're going'); south = toward the stern (the South Sea); spinward = the way the land turns (the direction of rotation); antispinward = against it; up = toward the Sunline; overhead / the Far Side = the land across the axis, 6 km up

**Regions:** the North Shore (the coast of the North Sea: harbours, beaches, headlands); the Northland (farms and towns between the North Shore and the Kettle); the Kettle Valley (the Kettle River and Lake Tamsin, round the middle of the world); the Southland (farms and towns between the Kettle and the South Shore); the South Shore (the coast of the South Sea: resort cities and cannery towns)

**Waters:** the North Sea: 1 km wide, round the whole bow end; cold, clear, fished; the South Sea: 1 km wide, round the stern end; warmer, resort water; the Kettle River: 0.5 km wide, circling the world in a wide basin; ships, barges, the Falls at Harrow Falls; Lake Tamsin: the widening of the Kettle: named for Tamsin Okafor-Reyes, the first child born aboard; creeks: Lost Creek, Mill Creek, Willow Run, Tanner Creek, Brush Creek, Cole Creek, Deer Creek, Sauk Creek, Heron Creek, Linden Run, Otter Run, Fox Run, Quarry Run, Sedge Run, Ash Run, Birch Run, Plum Run, Cedar Run

**Rail:** the Ring Line: one electric railway round the world with 146 level crossings

**Wildlife:** birds (sparrows, robins, swallows, herons, gulls, terns, wrens, larks, starlings), fish (perch, walleye, whitefish, bass, cod and haddock in the North Sea, snapper in the South), bees, rabbits, squirrels, frogs, turtles; no deer, no predators larger than a fox (the foxes were a VY 190 mistake)

**Livestock:** dairy cattle (small herds), pigs, chickens, sheep, goats; horses are few and prized

## What people know: the knowledge levels

- **living:** within living memory (VY 420+): people remember it or their parents told them
- **family:** family stories: known in the families concerned, patchy elsewhere
- **schooled:** taught in school: names and dates, little feeling
- **ritual:** kept by a holiday, a song or a church: known as a story, not as history
- **place:** survives as a place name or a monument; few know why
- **legend:** known, much distorted
- **scholar:** known only to Bellhaven scholars and the Wardens' Library circle
- **lost:** known to no one (the engine may reveal it)

**Memory model:** The 'floating gap' of oral history (Vansina, Oral Tradition as History, 1985): people know their origins as legend and the last three generations (~80-100 years, Assmann's communicative memory) as lived; the middle centuries blur. Here: the founding (VY 0-40) is legend and ritual; VY 40-420 is thin (a few schooled names, place names); VY 420-500 is living memory.
